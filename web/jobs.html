Queued jobs

What happens to the job?

Queue. Save the CSV to S3, write a job to SQS and record its status in DynamoDB.

Try and interrupt. A local worker receives the SQS message without deleting it. After the visibility timeout the job can be delivered again.

Complete. The worker invokes the same Lambda used in direct processing, checks its report, updates DynamoDB and deletes the SQS message.
